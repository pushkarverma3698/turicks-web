# Turicks — Narrative

Derives from [00-WORLD.md](00-WORLD.md). The Thread metaphor is mandatory in all story beats.

## Brand Narrative (the story we tell everywhere)

### The Three-Act Spine

**Act I — The Noise.**
Intelligence became cheap. Everyone can generate software now. Screens everywhere, output everywhere — and none of it holds weight. The world is loud with almost-working things.

**Act II — The Loom.**
Somewhere in Gurugram, a small team refused to ship noise. They treat machine intelligence the way a weaver treats raw silk: as material — powerful, but worthless until it's given structure. They pass chaos through the loom. What comes out is different: agents that actually close tickets, platforms that actually run schools, websites that actually sell.

**Act III — The Fabric.**
The businesses wearing Turicks fabric don't talk about AI. They talk about Tuesday: orders processed, students enrolled, invoices reconciled. The technology disappears into reliability. That disappearance is the product.

### Voice Rules

- Verbs of craft: *weave, forge, engineer, cut, temper, ship.* Never: *unlock, empower, revolutionize, supercharge, leverage.*
- Short declaratives. One idea per sentence. Understatement over hype.
- Numbers over adjectives: "closes 80% of tickets before a human sees them," not "dramatically reduces workload."
- We name the machines honestly. It's "an agent we built," never "magic."

## The 60-Second Brand Film — Story Beats

(Shot-level detail lives in [04-STORYBOARD.md](04-STORYBOARD.md).)

1. **0:00–0:10 — Noise.** Darkness. A rising wall of flickering, glitching generated interfaces — beautiful garbage. Sound: dense digital static.
2. **0:10–0:18 — The Thread appears.** One warm copper thread of light cuts through the wall. Static bends around it. Silence follows it.
3. **0:18–0:35 — The Loom works.** The thread moves through scenes of craft: a hand at a keyboard, a shuttle flying, circuitry being woven like textile, an agent's decision path lighting up node by node.
4. **0:35–0:50 — The Fabric holds.** The woven light becomes real things: a dashboard settling into place, a shopfront going live, a school platform filling with students, an invoice reconciling itself. Each lands with a soft, heavy *thunk* — weight, not sparkle.
5. **0:50–0:60 — End card.** The thread ties itself into the Turicks wordmark. Ink background. *"Woven, not generated."*

## Website as Narrative (scroll = film)

The homepage replays the three acts on scroll:

- **Hero = Act I→II:** noise field resolves as the Thread draws across the viewport, revealing the headline.
- **Services = Act II:** each service card is a loom-station the Thread passes through.
- **Case studies = Act III:** fabric swatches — each study a woven panel with its numbers embroidered in.
- **Contact = joining the weave:** the CTA line: *"Bring us the raw material."*
