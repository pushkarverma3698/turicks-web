# Turicks — Cinematic Storyboard

Brand film "**WOVEN**" — 60 seconds, 16:9 master (9:16 and 1:1 cutdowns defined at end). Beats from [01-NARRATIVE.md](01-NARRATIVE.md); look from [02-VISUAL-LANGUAGE.md](02-VISUAL-LANGUAGE.md); camera/tempo rules from [03-MOTION-LANGUAGE.md](03-MOTION-LANGUAGE.md).

Sound design spine: dense digital static (Act I) → static dies to near-silence with a single warm hum following the Thread (Act II) → soft heavy *thunk* on each landing (Act III) → silence + one final thunk on end card.

---

## ACT I — THE NOISE (0:00–0:10)

### Shot 01 — "Wall of Almost"
- **0:00–0:06 · Locked-off wide**
- Pure black frame. Flickers begin: dozens of ghostly generated UIs — landing pages, chatbots, dashboards — stuttering in and out, overlapping, slightly wrong (misaligned text, garbled labels). Cold, desaturated, blue-grey. Density builds until the frame is a wall of glitching noise.
- Audio: static and overlapping synthetic voices, rising.
- VO (optional, low, flat): *"Anyone can generate software now."*

### Shot 02 — "The Cut"
- **0:06–0:10 · Locked-off, same framing**
- A single molten-copper thread of light enters frame left, mid-height, traveling right with absolute straightness. Where it passes, the glitching UIs behind it dim, freeze, and fall dark. It exits frame right leaving a horizontal wake of clean black.
- Audio: static cuts to silence along the thread's wake; a warm low hum remains.
- VO: *"Almost none of it holds."*

## ACT II — THE LOOM (0:10–0:35)

### Shot 03 — "Hands"
- **0:10–0:16 · Macro, slow 3% push-in**
- Extreme close-up: a hand at a mechanical keyboard in near-darkness, lit only by warm copper light from screen-left. Skin texture, dust motes in the beam. On a keypress, the Thread emerges from beneath the keycap and flows off-frame right.
- Audio: one keypress, close-miked, reverberant.

### Shot 04 — "The Shuttle Flies"
- **0:16–0:22 · Locked-off medium**
- A loom shuttle — but machined from dark anodized metal, carrying the copper thread as its weft — fires across warp threads made of fine fiber-optic strands. The weave visibly grows one row. Ink background, single warm key light.
- Audio: the shuttle's *snap*, then the loom's heavy soft *thunk* as the beater packs the row.

### Shot 05 — "Circuit Textile"
- **0:22–0:28 · Top-down macro, locked-off**
- The woven fabric in close-up: it is simultaneously textile and circuit board — copper traces as weft threads through a dark woven substrate. Current pulses travel the traces like the shuttle: still, then sudden travel, then still.
- Audio: hum deepens; faint tick of pulses.

### Shot 06 — "The Decision Path"
- **0:28–0:35 · Slow 2% push-in on dark space**
- Abstract: an agent's reasoning as weaving. Dim bone-white nodes hang in ink like warp threads seen end-on. The Thread enters and weaves through them — under, over, under — lighting each node it touches. Wrong branches stay dark. One continuous line finds the path.
- Audio: soft ticks per node; hum rising in warmth.
- VO: *"We treat intelligence as raw material. Powerful. Worthless until it's given structure."*

## ACT III — THE FABRIC (0:35–0:50)

Each shot: the Thread enters frame left, ties into the scene, and the scene *lands* with a thunk. Real, grounded, no floating holograms — screens exist on desks, in rooms, at night, warm-lit.

### Shot 07 — "The Dashboard Settles"
- **0:35–0:40 · Locked-off over-shoulder, dark office**
- A real monitor on a real desk. The Thread runs along the desk edge into the screen's bezel; on the screen an operations dashboard assembles row by row — like rows packed by a beater — and settles. A number ticks to its final value and holds: `1,240 orders reconciled`.
- Audio: *thunk*.

### Shot 08 — "The School Fills"
- **0:40–0:45 · Locked-off wide, evening classroom office**
- A school administrator's screen: enrollment platform, seats filling as woven cells. Outside the window, dusk over Gurugram — sodium lights, real skyline, no sci-fi. Thread runs along the window frame into the device.
- Audio: *thunk*; distant city.

### Shot 09 — "The Shop Goes Live"
- **0:45–0:50 · Macro on a phone held in a hand**
- A shopkeeper's hand holds a phone; a storefront site (BizSite AI) finishes weaving itself — hero image settling, prices embroidering in mono type. Thumb taps **Publish**. The Thread exits the phone's edge and leaves frame right.
- Audio: final *thunk*, then silence.
- VO: *"The businesses we build for don't talk about AI. They talk about Tuesday."*

## END CARD (0:50–0:60)

### Shot 10 — "The Knot"
- **0:50–1:00 · Locked-off, centered**
- Ink void. The Thread enters left, and in one continuous motion ties itself into a knot that becomes the crossbar of the **T** in TURICKS. Wordmark in Bone settles behind it (`--ease-settle`). Below, in Instrument Serif italic: *Woven, not generated.* Hold 4 full seconds. Mono URL bottom-center: `turicks.com`.
- Audio: one last soft thunk as the knot cinches; room tone; out.
