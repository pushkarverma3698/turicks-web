# Turicks Creative System — External Review Board Findings (2035)

Board: former Vision Pro design lead · Awwwards jury president · Anduril CD · Obsidian lead experience architect · Disney Imagineer · former Stripe design head · principal spatial designer · film director · HCI researcher.

Mandate: destroy weak ideas. Nothing here is softened.

---

## THE HEADLINE FINDING

**The brand says "Woven, not generated" — and every image in the pipeline is AI-generated.**

This is not a nitpick. It is a loaded gun aimed at the brand's own head. The day a competitor or journalist notices that the agency selling craft-over-generation shipped a Midjourney site, the tagline becomes a punchline. The 10x competitor doesn't need their budget to beat this — they need one tweet.

Every decision below flows from fixing this and four other structural gaps:

1. **No intelligence in the experience.** Turicks builds AI agents. The site contains zero live intelligence. It's a museum about weaving with no weaver. (Stripe: "You're a payments company that doesn't take payments on its own homepage.")
2. **The metaphor has no intellectual spine.** The Loom is an aesthetic. It's missing its historical fact: **the Jacquard loom was the first programmable machine — computing literally descends from weaving.** This one true sentence turns a pretty metaphor into an inevitable one, and nobody in the docs found it.
3. **The world is premium 2026, not 2035.** Dark mode + one accent + scroll-rail camera + localStorage = the current Awwwards meta, executed well. 2035-ness requires a world that is *alive*: live intelligence, collective persistence, things that exist because other humans were here.
4. **Nothing to take home.** Visitors watch; they don't keep. No artifact = no sharing = no virality. People share things that are *theirs*.

## PER-DOCUMENT SCORES

Criteria: memorability / originality / emotional impact / spatial readiness / award potential / brand uniqueness / product differentiation / 2035-proofing / feasibility / shareability.

### 00-WORLD.md — 7.2/10
- **Strong:** darkness-default physics; craft-marks rule; anti-cyberpunk stance; Gurugram honesty. Feasibility 9, uniqueness 8.
- **Weaknesses:** 2035-proofing 5 — the world is a look, not a living system. No answer to "who operates the loom?" No intelligence, no other people, no time.
- **Generic patterns:** "chaos → order via signature light element" is the default AI-brand world since 2023.
- **Clichés:** "thread of light through darkness" — a glowing line in the dark is fintech-hero-section furniture. The *weave and tension* are ownable; the line alone is not.
- **Missed:** the Jacquard lineage (fatal miss); the Weaver; collective fabric.

### 01-NARRATIVE.md — 6.8/10
- **Strong:** "Woven, not generated" is a 9/10 tagline — keep untouched. Voice rules and banned-word list are genuinely good. "They talk about Tuesday" is the best line in the system.
- **Weaknesses:** emotional impact 5 — the three-act spine (noise → refuge of craft → quiet reliability) is the plot of every AI-agency film this decade. Act I opens on a complaint about the industry, which is a weak, defensive opening.
- **Clichés:** "wall of glitching interfaces" opener — the board has seen this exact cold open ~40 times on award reels.
- **Missed:** opening with the historical thunderbolt instead: *"The first program was woven."* That's an opening that makes judges sit up.

### 02-VISUAL-LANGUAGE.md — 7.5/10
- **Strong:** copper-scarcity rule is real art direction; no-bloom/no-glassmorphism bans show taste; token discipline is executable.
- **Weaknesses:** originality 6 — ink + single warm accent + serif/sans/mono triad is the 2024–26 "editorial premium dark" template (Linear, Vercel-adjacent agencies, half of last year's SOTD list). Well-chosen, not new.
- **Missed:** a material innovation. The palette is flat pigment; a woven brand should have a *structural color* rule — color that changes with viewing angle like real thread (feasible: anisotropic sheen tokens for 3D, subtle duotone-shift on 2D hover). Nobody owns that.

### 03-MOTION-LANGUAGE.md — 8.0/10 (best document)
- **Strong:** the loom rhythm (still → shuttle → settle) is a genuinely distinctive motion thesis; "page is still after load" is brave and correct; banning springs is correct.
- **Weaknesses:** shareability 5 — motion grammar is felt, not clipped.
- **Missed:** motion as *information* — the rhythm never encodes meaning (e.g., system status, weave progress). It decorates; it could speak.

### 04-STORYBOARD.md — 6.0/10 (weakest creative document)
- **Weaknesses:** emotional impact 5, originality 5. Act I is the glitch-wall cliché. Act III is three consecutive shots of *screens on desks* — the film about transcending software ends by photographing monitors. The Imagineer: "Your finale is a man looking at a dashboard. I would not build that ride."
- **Generic:** number-ticks-to-final-value; dusk-skyline-behind-window; thumb-taps-publish.
- **Strong:** the end-card knot; the sound spine; VO restraint; cut-on-thunk discipline.
- **Missed:** the film never shows the one image only Turicks can own (see 12-SIGNATURE-SHOT). And no *people affected* — fabric is worn by bodies, software is worn by businesses; we never see the wearing.

### 05-PROMPTS.md — 7.0/10, with a red flag
- **Strong:** global style block + negative prompt discipline is professional; no-text-in-assets rule is correct.
- **Red flag:** "macro woven circuit board with glowing copper traces" is *Midjourney catnip* — among the most-generated AI art motifs of the decade. The system's hero texture is the most AI-looking image class in existence. Combined with the headline finding, this is the doc most likely to sink the brand.
- **Fix:** shoot the four hero textiles **physically** (macro photography of real copper thread woven on a tabletop loom — days of work, trivial cost, total narrative armor: "we wove our textures"). Generate only the impossible shots (F02, F06, F10 abstractions).

### 06-ASSET-INVENTORY.md — 7.5/10
- **Strong:** status pipeline, acceptance criteria, "could this exist on a desk in Gurugram" test.
- **Missed:** no artifact assets (nothing a visitor keeps), no AR/spatial deliverables (USDZ knot), no sonic logo as a first-class asset, no physical-shoot line items.

### 07-EXECUTOR-INSTRUCTIONS.md — 8.0/10
- **Strong:** "docs outrank your taste," phase gates with evidence, escalation triggers.
- **Missed:** no *visual* verification — everything gates on commands and checklists, nothing gates on "does the frame actually look right." An average developer will pass every gate and still ship a mediocre frame. (Addressed in 14-EXECUTION-RISK-REPORT.)

### 08-SPATIAL-COMPUTING-LAYER.md — 7.8/10
- **Strong:** architecture is the most technically credible spatial spec the board has seen from an agency this size (feasibility 9); the thread-as-lighting-rig idea is excellent; the SVG→3D custody handoff is award-detail; hero moment ("your scroll weaves the room brighter") is the system's best experiential idea.
- **Weaknesses:** originality 6 — one rail, one corridor, forward only. It's a beautiful hallway. No secrets, no volume, no reason to return. Memory moment is localStorage — device-bound, deletable, and *private*; a loom is a communal machine. 2035-proofing 6.
- **Missed:** the Weaver (live agent visibly operating the loom); collective world-state; anything the visitor *finds* rather than receives.

### CLAUDE.md — 8.0/10. Does its job. Needs the new docs registered and the physical-shoot rule added.

### PHASE-0-BLUEPRINT.md — 7.0/10
- **Weaknesses:** internally inconsistent — 08 declares itself "Phase 7" but the blueprint has six phases. No phase for the physical shoot. No taste-collapse gates (only build gates). No budget/scope note on the one backend exception the strongest ideas require.

**System average: 7.3 — an excellent 2026 website. Not yet a memorable world.**

## HOW THE 10x COMPETITOR BEATS THIS

1. **They build a real loom.** A physical, Jacquard-descended machine weaving actual conductive copper cloth, filmed and volumetrically scanned. Physical truth versus our CG metaphor. Their behind-the-scenes content alone out-shares our entire film.
2. **They staff the site with a live agent** — visitors watch real intelligence make real decisions in real time. Product as experience.
3. **They make it collective** — a persistent world visibly accumulating every visitor's presence. Ours resets per device.
4. **They give everyone an artifact** — a generated-for-you object people post without being asked.
5. **They open a physical room** in their city; the website is its shadow.

**Our counter (this is the good news):** items 2–4 cost almost nothing in money — they cost *ideas*, and Turicks's actual product (agents) is item 2. We cannot out-spend; we can out-truth. Item 1 we can execute at tabletop scale for the price of a camera weekend. The revision plan below does exactly this.

## PHASE 2 — CORE ASSUMPTIONS, CHALLENGED

**Is the Thread enough?** No. The Thread is a pen, not a story. Ownable elements, ranked: (1) *tension* — the pull interaction, keep and elevate; (2) *the weave* — collective, accumulating; (3) the thread — demote to connective tissue. The signature is **what the thread does under load**, not its glow.

**Is the Loom metaphor deep enough?** The metaphor is deep; the *use* of it was shallow. The fix is the Jacquard fact: punch cards → Babbage → computing. "Software returning to the loom" stops being poetic and becomes *literal*. This must enter 00 and 01 as the spine.

**Too linear?** Yes. One rail = one visit. Fixed by 09-DISCOVERY-LAYER (off-rail volume, secrets, reasons to return).

**2035 or premium 2026?** Premium 2026. The three missing 2035 properties: **alive** (live agent), **collective** (shared persistent fabric), **generative-personal** (artifacts made for you). All three are now specified (09, 11, 12).

**Actively shareable moments?** Previously zero. Now: the Naming artifact (12), three clip-grade impossible moments (10), the selvage zoom (09).

**A true "holy shit" moment?** Now yes — the signature shot (12): a machine of light weaves *your name* into cloth that stays in the world for everyone after you. Personal + permanent + witnessed.

**What do Awwwards judges remember in 12 months?** Not palettes. They remember: "the site where you weave your name into the fabric and it's still there when you come back," "the pull-back where the whole world turns out to be one thread of a bigger loom," and "the agency whose textures were woven on a real loom." Those three now exist.
